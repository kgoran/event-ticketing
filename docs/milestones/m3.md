  asked for M3 to M5 without approval round-trips ("no need to get approval from me, just start

## Goal

The lab keeps serving when a part of it fails. The database runs as a primary, a synchronous
standby and a read replica on three nodes labelled as zones; killing the primary under load
causes under 60 s of errors and breaks none of the T2 invariants (T10). Browsing reads from the
replica and a shared cache in Valkey, so a hot on-sale doesn't reach the primary for reads. The
fan site runs as several instances. Prometheus, Loki, Tempo and Grafana show each service, and
PublicApi's autoscaler moves to its proper signal. At the door, a scanner admits ticket holders
offline and syncs when it can (T8).

## Definition of done

1. `dotnet run lab.cs -- up` builds the whole M3 lab from nothing in under 15 minutes on the
   presenter's laptop (16 GB for Docker).
2. Killing the primary under load: under 60 s of errors, no broken invariant (T10). Stopping a
   zone's node: the system keeps serving, the database within 5 minutes (§10).
3. Browsing is served from the cache and the replica; the primary's CPU doesn't rise with it.
4. The fan site runs twice and keeps fans signed in through a deploy.
5. Grafana shows every service; PublicApi scales on utilization.
6. A scanner admits a ticket offline; T8's numbers hold for 200 simulated devices.
7. A backup restores.
8. Every new decision has an ADR.

## Risks

- **Memory.** M2 used about 7 GB, 3 of them Keycloak's uncapped heap. Capping Keycloak and
  dropping the Aspire Dashboard pay for most of M3; three nodes, three database instances and the
  observability stack cost about 3–4 GB more. On a laptop with less, run one node (ADR-0020).
- **PgBouncer in transaction mode** breaks session state: anything that relies on a session
  (prepared statements kept across transactions, `LISTEN`, session advisory locks) must not.
- **The scanner's camera** needs a browser that grants it on `localhost`; typing a code is the
  fallback.
- **Holds on new instances are slow** (found in AS2): the warm-up covers browsing only, and a
  burst onto instances that have all just started times holds out for a minute. Warming the hold
  path is open, and T11's verification in M5 needs it ([lab](../lab.md#autoscaling)).
